# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
All full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
credited at a rate of 1.67 days per completed month of service. Leave requests
must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance and are
subject to manager approval. Unused annual leave may be carried over into the
next calendar year up to a maximum of 5 days; any balance beyond that is
forfeited unless local law requires otherwise.

## Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per year. For absences of three or
more consecutive days, a medical certificate must be provided to HR. Sick
leave does not need to be requested in advance, but employees should notify
their manager as early as possible on the day of absence.

## Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year, as published annually on
the HR portal. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a
compensatory day off within the following month.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Parental leave
requests should be submitted to HR at least 8 weeks before the expected start
date.

## Unpaid Leave
Employees who have exhausted their paid leave balances may request unpaid
leave of up to 30 days per year, subject to manager and HR approval.

# Code of Conduct

## Purpose
This Code of Conduct outlines the standards of behavior expected of all
employees, contractors, and interns while representing the company, whether
on company premises, at company-sponsored events, or online.

## Professional Behavior
Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with
respect and courtesy. Discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind —
based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any
other protected characteristic — will not be tolerated and may result in
disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or business relationships
that could reasonably be seen as creating a conflict of interest with their
role. Disclosures should be made to a direct manager or HR as soon as the
conflict is identified.

## Confidentiality
Employees must protect confidential company information, including customer
data, trade secrets, and internal business plans, both during and after their
employment. Confidential information should never be shared outside the
company without prior written authorization.

## Use of Company Resources
Company equipment, software, and network access are provided for business
purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere
with work responsibilities, violate any policy, or incur unreasonable cost to
the company.

## Reporting Violations
Employees who witness or suspect a violation of this Code of Conduct should
report it to their manager, HR, or through the confidential ethics hotline.
Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is strictly
prohibited.
